## What Stack Drop collects

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A random account ID | Automatically, the first time the game goes online | To keep your progress and connect your scores and friends to you |
| Your Google account email | Only if you choose **Sign in with Google** | So you can restore your progress on another device |
| The display name and avatar you choose | Only if you choose a name | Shown on leaderboards and to your friends |
| Your friend code, friends and friend requests | When you use Friends | To let you add friends and compare scores |
| Game progress: best scores, gems, unlocked themes, settings, daily gift streak | While you play | To back up your progress |
| Run statistics: score, duration, number of drops and perfect drops | When a run is sent to the leaderboard | To check that scores are genuine |
| Device integrity information (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity) | When the game talks to its servers | To block fake or modified copies of the game |
| Advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device and app information | By Google AdMob, only after you give any consent required in your region | To show ads and measure them |

Stack Drop never shows or stores your Google profile photo, has no chat, and does not ask for
your real name, location, contacts or photos.

## Who can see what

- **Everyone signed in to the game** can see your display name, avatar and best scores on the
  leaderboards and on your public profile.
- **Your friends** see the same, plus that you are friends.
- **Only you** can read your saved progress.
- **Google** processes the data on our behalf: Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore,
  Cloud Functions, App Check) stores and serves it, and AdMob serves ads. See
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

We do not sell your data.

## Ads and consent

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and
Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent
message. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**. You can also
reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete it at any time:

- **In the game:** Profile → **Delete account**. This immediately and permanently deletes your
  profile, name, friend code, friendships, friend requests, leaderboard entries, saved progress and
  account, and resets the game on your phone (your sound and motion settings are kept).
- **Without the app:** follow the steps at [account-deletion page URL].

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone, but not your online account; use
one of the options above for that.

## Children

Stack Drop is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Server access is limited by security rules so players can
only read their own private data, and every change to public data goes through validated
server code.
