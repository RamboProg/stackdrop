# Delete your Stack Drop account

*Published at <https://stackdrop-towers.vercel.app/delete-account> from
`site/delete-account/index.html`; keep both in sync.*

You can delete your Stack Drop account and all of its data at any time.

## From the game (fastest)

1. Open Stack Drop.
2. Tap your avatar at the top-left of the menu to open **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** at the bottom and confirm twice.

Deletion happens immediately.

## Without the app

Email **stackdrop.game@gmail.com** from any address with the subject **"Delete my Stack Drop account"** and
include your **friend code** (shown on the Profile screen, like `K7Q2-M9XD`) or the **email of the
Google account** you signed in with. We delete the account within **30 days** and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your account and account ID
- Your display name, avatar and friend code
- Your friendships and friend requests (on both sides)
- Your leaderboard entries
- Your saved progress (gems, themes, best scores, settings)
- Internal records used to check scores

Nothing is kept afterwards. Crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted
automatically after 90 days. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's
own policies (<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>); you can reset your advertising ID
in your device settings.
