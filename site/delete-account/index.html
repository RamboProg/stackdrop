Delete your Stack Drop account

Stack Drop by GrabStacks Studio

You can delete your Stack Drop account and all of its data at any time.

From the game (fastest)

  1. Open Stack Drop.
  2. Tap your avatar at the top-left of the menu to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm twice.

Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

If you signed in with Google: email stackdrop.game@gmail.com from that Google account's address with the subject "Delete my Stack Drop account". Sending from the account's own address shows the request is really yours. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you never signed in with Google (a guest account): open the game and use Delete account as above. If you no longer have the game, there is nothing that links the account to you, so it can't be found by request; instead it is deleted automatically after 12 months without use, together with everything listed below.

What is deleted

  • Your account and account ID
  • Your display name, avatar and friend code
  • Your friendships and friend requests (on both sides)
  • Your leaderboard entries
  • Your saved progress (gems, themes, best scores, settings)
  • Internal records used to check scores (when your runs started, how many you sent)

Nothing else is kept afterwards, except: a marker holding only your account ID for up to 2 hours, so that a save already on its way can't recreate your data; and our server logs, which record your account ID when it is deleted and are kept by Google Cloud for 30 days. Crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 90 days. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's own policies (policies.google.com/technologies/ads); you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
