Stack Drop never shows or stores your Google profile photo, has no chat, and does not ask for your real name, location, contacts or photos.

Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in to the game can see your display name, avatar and best scores on the leaderboards and on your public profile.
  • Your friends see the same, plus that you are friends.
  • Only you can read your saved progress.
  • Google processes the data on our behalf: Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Crashlytics) stores and serves it, and AdMob serves ads. See policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your data.

Ads and consent

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the game: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, name, friend code, friendships, friend requests, leaderboard entries, saved progress and account, and resets the game on your phone (your sound and motion settings are kept).
  • Without the app: follow the steps at stackdrop-towers.vercel.app/delete-account.

Crash reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your phone, but not your online account; use one of the options above for that.

Children

Stack Drop is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Server access is limited by security rules so players can only read their own private data, and every change to public data goes through validated server code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
